# INTRODUCTION

## What Are Brand Guidelines?

Brand guidelines are a set of rules and instructions that define how a brand should be presented visually and verbally. They serve as a reference document for designers, marketing professionals, and anyone involved in creating content for the brand.

These guidelines ensure consistency across all brand communications, maintain brand integrity, and help establish a strong, recognizable brand identity.

By following brand guidelines, Elite Realty can effectively communicate its values, build trust with clients, and create a cohesive brand experience.

# CONCEPT

The Elite Realty visual identity is conceived as a representation of **sophistication, clarity, and modern luxury**—capturing the essence of high-end real estate without pretense.

We adopt a **minimalist approach** where simplicity is paramount, complemented by strategic use of contrast and refined typography. These elements combine harmoniously to create a sense of exclusivity and trust.

The dark color palette signals confidence and sophistication, breaking from traditional real estate aesthetics while maintaining timeless appeal. Gold accents add warmth and luxury without overwhelming the clean foundation.

**Keywords:** Sophisticated | Minimalist | Confident | Clean | Luxurious

---

# LOGO SUITE

The logo suite includes variations and adaptations of the primary logo to ensure coherence and versatility across different contexts and media. These variations facilitate adaptation to different backgrounds and spaces, guaranteeing legibility and recognition in any situation.

## Primary Logo
**ELITE REALTY** wordmark in clean, sophisticated typography. Used for large applications where the logo has prominent placement.

## Secondary Logo
**ER** monogram for compact applications. Maintains brand recognition in smaller spaces like social media profiles, favicons, and watermarks.

## Tagline Version
Primary logo with tagline: **"Luxury Real Estate | Puerto Rico & Miami"**

# LOGO SPECIFICATIONS

## Primary Wordmark

```
ELITE REALTY
```

- Clean sans-serif or refined serif typography
- Generous letter-spacing (tracking)
- All caps for authority
- Weight: Light to Regular for elegance

## Monogram (ER)

- Interlocking or stacked E and R
- Geometric, balanced design
- Works at small sizes
- Maintains elegance at scale

---

# CLEAR SPACE

The clear space around the logo is the minimum area that must remain free to ensure legibility and visual impact. This is important for preserving brand integrity and avoiding visual interference that could affect recognition.

**Minimum clear space:** Height of the "E" in ELITE on all sides

---

# POSITIVE / NEGATIVE

## Light Backgrounds
- Black (#0a0a0a) logo on white/light backgrounds
- Gold (#d4af37) logo as accent option

## Dark Backgrounds
- White (#ffffff) logo on dark backgrounds
- Gold (#d4af37) logo as accent option

---

# CORRECT & INCORRECT USAGE

## ✓ Correct Usage
- Use the logo with correct proportions
- Respect the clear space zone
- Use approved color combinations with sufficient contrast
- Place logo in visually balanced positions

## ✗ Incorrect Usage
- Do NOT alter proportions, angles, or spacing
- Do NOT use colors outside the brand palette
- Do NOT use low-contrast color combinations
- Do NOT crowd the logo with other elements
- Do NOT add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)

# BRAND ELEMENTS

Brand elements are visual components used to represent the brand in a unique and distinctive way. They are specifically designed to reflect the personality and values of the brand, strengthen visual identity, and aid recognition among the target audience.

---

## Photography Style

### Property Photography
- Clean, well-lit interiors
- Emphasis on architectural details
- Minimal staging, let space speak
- Natural light preferred
- Wide angles for sense of space

### Neighborhood Photography
- Aerial/drone shots for neighborhoods section
- Capture lifestyle and environment
- Ocean, beach, mountain vistas
- Golden hour lighting

### Portrait Photography
- Professional headshot of Alexandra
- Clean background (white or dark)
- Approachable yet professional expression
- High-quality, contemporary style

---

## Iconography

- Thin, elegant line weights
- Geometric, minimal style
- Consistent stroke width
- Gold or white on dark backgrounds
- Examples: bed, bath, square footage, location pin

---

## Patterns & Textures

- Minimal use of patterns
- Subtle geometric lines if needed
- Avoid busy or decorative patterns
- Let negative space breathe
